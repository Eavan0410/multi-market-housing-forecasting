## Taiwan iMaPP × manual timeline spot check (2020–2023)

**Input vintage and coverage.** The `iMaPP_database-2024-12-2.xlsx` data come from the IMF December 2024 vintage (the accompanying `ReadMe_Main.txt` says it extends to 2023M12, with 2021 refinements and historical corrections). In sheet `MaPP`, Taiwan is `iso3=TWN`, source country `TaiwanProvinceofChina`. All 48 monthly observations from January 2020 to December 2023 contain numerical **zero**, not blank/NA, in `LTV` (column Z) and `LoanR_HH` (column W). The corresponding country cells on the instrument-specific narrative sheets (`LTV` and `LoanR_HH`, column BF) have no text for these months.

| Manual ID | Announcement date | Manual direction | Manual event / proposed mapping | iMaPP for that month (`LTV`, `LoanR_HH`) | Assessment |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| E001 | 2020-12-07 | +2 | Mortgage LTV limits for corporate purchasers, third homes, land and developers | (0, 0) | Unmatched LTV-related tightening |
| E002 | 2021-03-18 | +2 | Lower mortgage LTV limits | (0, 0) | Unmatched LTV-related tightening |
| E003 | 2021-09-23 | +1 | Mortgage grace-period prohibition and tighter land-related loan caps | (0, 0) | Unmatched LTV / potentially LoanR-related tightening; instrument assignment may overlap |
| E006 | 2021-12-16 | +2 | Lower loan ratios for luxury/multiple homes, land and developers | (0, 0) | Unmatched LTV-related tightening |
| E009 | 2023-06-15 | +1 | 70% second-home mortgage LTV cap in major cities | (0, 0) | Unmatched LTV-related tightening |
| E025 | 2023-07-13 | −2 | Subsidized first-home lending, larger loans, longer terms / grace periods | (0, 0) | Unmatched observation, but not established as eligible for either iMaPP instrument |

**2022:** Neither the manual timeline's `selective_credit` class nor iMaPP's two target instruments record a policy action; this is agreement *within these two source definitions*, not proof of no housing-policy changes. No iMaPP-only nonzero LTV or LoanR_HH months were found in the entire 2020–2023 check window.

**Interpretation / decision.** For the five LTV-related manual events, the discrepancy is confirmed at the source-record level. This does **not** establish an error by IMF: the sources may differ in inclusion rules, survey reporting, and implementation-versus-announcement timing, and the ±2 manual intensity score is not comparable numerically with the iMaPP ±1 action indicator. Keep the original datasets untouched; report Taiwan iMaPP coverage as incomplete relative to this independently assembled announcement timeline. Confirm E025's instrument eligibility separately if it will be included in a future agreement-rate calculation. Do not change the experiment feature or code as part of this evidence-only spot check.

**Evidence:** iMaPP workbook `MaPP!W47690:W47737` (`LoanR_HH`), `MaPP!Z47690:Z47737` (`LTV`), and the narrative cells `LoanR_HH!BF364:BF411` / `LTV!BF364:BF411`; `data/raw/policy/manual_timeline.csv` entries E001, E002, E003, E006, E009, E025; iMaPP `TOC` definitions and uploaded `ReadMe_Main.txt`.
